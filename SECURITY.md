# Security policy

Groovy Security builds Whiteout AI, and we take the security of our products seriously. If you believe you have found a security vulnerability, please tell us privately.

## How to report

Email security@groovysec.com (or support@groovysec.com). Please include:

- the product and version, or the URL, that is affected
- what the issue is and what an attacker could do with it
- steps to reproduce it, and any proof-of-concept code or screenshots
- how you would like to be credited, if at all

Do not open a public GitHub issue for security problems.

## What to expect

- We acknowledge reports within 2 business days.
- We give you an initial assessment within 5 business days, and an update at least every 14 days.
- We aim to fix critical issues within 48 hours, and others within 30 to 180 days depending on severity.
- We agree a disclosure date with you and credit you if you would like.

## Safe harbor

We will not take legal action against good-faith research that follows this policy. Please:

- test only accounts you own
- do not access or change other people's data
- avoid denial-of-service and social-engineering tests
- give us reasonable time to fix an issue before you publish it

## Scope

In scope: the Whiteout AI service and API, Desktop Guard, the browser extension, the IDE plugins, the infrastructure agent, the SDKs and installer, the license API, groovysec.com and the partner portal.

Out of scope:

- third-party services we use (report those to the vendor)
- deployments customers run in their own cloud accounts
- denial of service, spam and social engineering
- automated scanner output without demonstrated impact

We do not run a paid bug bounty.
